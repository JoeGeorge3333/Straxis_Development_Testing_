Brand Positioning

What STRAXIS Feels Like

STRAXIS is not soft motivation. It is a premium accountability environment built around pressure, ritual, and competitive proof. The tone should feel composed and elite, never cartoonishly intense.

Forged

Textures, contrast, and language should suggest grit and heat, but remain clean and intentional.

Competitive

Ranking, point totals, streaks, and gain or loss should feel central, not secondary.

Premium

Dark surfaces, controlled spacing, and restrained highlights make the product feel elevated rather than gamified.

Focused

The MVP supports one loop. Avoid decorative features that dilute the tension of daily completion.

Layout Principles

How Screens Should Be Structured

The interface should feel like a control panel, not a social feed clone. Use hierarchy that keeps score and status near the top of the viewport, with actions following immediately below.

1. Lead with the score

The first thing users should see is where they stand today and how much ground remains.

2. Keep the next action obvious

If a workout or habit is incomplete, the action to close it should be visible without hunting.

3. Treat ranking as motivation

The leaderboard is not a separate utility screen. It is emotional fuel for the entire product.

4. Show less, mean more

MVP screens should be tight and intentional. Avoid filler modules, empty graphs, and decorative placeholders.

Do and Don’t

  • Do keep gold for actions, wins, and important metrics.
  • Do use strong headlines and concise copy.
  • Do make cards feel dense and controlled.
  • Do not use pastel gradients, soft wellness language, or playful iconography.
  • Do not overuse charts when a single number creates more tension.
  • Do not fill empty space with generic community features in the MVP.
